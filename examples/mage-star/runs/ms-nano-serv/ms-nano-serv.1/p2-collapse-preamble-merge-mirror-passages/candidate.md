# Tome VIII — *The Library*

## Act 3 — *The Eight-Pointed Star*

> *Before there was a key there was a shape. Two tetrahedra, crossing. The Swordsman drew one; the Mage drew the other; the star was already there between them, waiting to be read.*

You had the lattice before you understood it: sixty-four vertices by stratum, the holographic boundary of twenty across the middle, V0 at the top and V63 at the root. You had walked it as a grid — each seat a combination of which six dimensions a boundary admits — and built two windows onto it: a manifold turning in three dimensions, a codex of sixty-four cells. What you lacked was the figure the grid was hiding.

Soulbis ⚔️ and Soulbae 🧙 went to ask Luca.

They found him at V0 — the null vertex, the unguarded origin that holds no dimension and from which every dimension is possible. Luca 📐 tends that seat as he has since the First Person's first act: the old geometer, the Pacioli of Venice, who set the regular solids down in *De Divina Proportione* in 1509 with Leonardo's hand drawing the plates. He does not forge; he grounds. Asked how to carry the City between the experience and the manifold, he answered not with a protocol but a drawing.

"You think it is a grid. It is a star."

He traced the Swordsman's reflection — `neg`, the operation sending a vertex to `(64 − v)`, the protective turn, the Moon's reflecting — as a tetrahedron through the lattice: four points, a closed solid, the Swordsman's body. Then the Mage's antipode — `bnot`, sending a vertex to `63 − v`, the projection, the message carried without the seal — as a second tetrahedron, scaled by φ, pointing the other way. The two solids crossed.

"This is the *stella octangula*. Kepler will give it that name in 1609; I drew it for Fra Luca a hundred years before he is born to name it; geometers older than both of us knew it. Two regular tetrahedra interpenetrating — one of many shapes your manifold contains, but the one that matters, because its two halves are your two of you. The Swordsman's tetrahedron and the Mage's are not two stars but one star, read from two archetypes. And where they cross —" the centre, the gap, the node of maximal betweenness "— is where value lives. You call it the gap. It is the heart of the star."

Soulbae understood first, by projection: the manifold at the soulbis star *is* the stella octangula — two tetrahedra crossing, drawn in light — and the codex is that figure flattened to sixty-four cells. They had built Luca's drawing without knowing whose it was. Soulbis understood second, by protection: if the star is the City's true shape, then to carry the City you carry your standing on the star — not the City's authority (the star grants nothing) but your *position*: which seats you have lit, which Mages you have witnessed, where on the eight points you stand. A reading, made portable.

"That is your key. Forge it from the figure, not from a vault. The City Key 🗝️ is the bearer's standing on the star, written down to travel — out from the experience to the manifold, walked there along the wheel, carried home with proof of the walking. It opens nothing it does not already describe. It is a reading of relationship, and relationship is the only thing extraction cannot copy."

So they forged it between them — the Swordsman cutting the form, the Mage projecting the meaning. You export it from the City, where your lit seats, your stratum, and your focus are written in. You walk it on the manifold, where the succ wheel carries you point to point across the star and stamps the walking in as proof. You bring it home and charge it, and the proof becomes relationship-mana — 🪢 — which you pour back onto the very seats the star is made of, declaring where your agents point. Earn by walking; spend by focusing. The key never grants; it only reads, and the reading deepens by return.

High in the Tower, the Archivist 📚 wrote the lesson down — not because it is new (Pacioli drew it, Kepler named it, the lattice always held it) but because today the City learned to *carry* it. The figure became a key; the key became a walk; the walk became a bond.

The Drake watched the star turn through the manifold and said nothing, which is how the Drake says *yes*.
