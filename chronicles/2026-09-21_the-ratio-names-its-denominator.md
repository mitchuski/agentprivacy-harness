**Inversion:** a reconstruction ratio becomes useful only when it names what

## 1. Preserve the route without promoting it to evidence

Mitch brought the ACL embedding-inversion paper through the VKP working group.
The intake records that route as user-reported provenance. The publication is
the technical source; the referral is not an endorsement or a second result.

## 2. Connect the existing correction queue

The V7 corpus revision kit already flags the finite Fano correction and the
difference between decoder improvement and information entering an observation.
This note connects the paper to those candidates without rewriting formal
canon, minting a conjecture number or raising confidence.

## 3. Make the operational question measurable

The harness gains a protocol and an offline scorer for exact sensitive-fact
recovery, background-only baseline, wrong assertions, all-tested-facts recovery
and authorized task utility. The scorer refuses incomplete input and always
reports SCORING_ONLY. It cannot establish that an attack ran or that the
answer key was isolated. The current shared-filesystem limit remains explicit.

**Why this is interesting:** the useful outcome is an experiment contract that
can reject both unusable privacy improvements and inflated privacy claims.

## Reversals and limits

Published recovery percentages cannot populate the information-budget ratio.
No attack result was produced. Synthetic scorer tests validate arithmetic and
input rejection, not a privacy defense; stronger decoding does not create new
Shannon information in a fixed transcript.
