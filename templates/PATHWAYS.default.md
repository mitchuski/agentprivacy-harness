## Bringing a fold back

A fold made here is yours. If you want it seen: seal it (`node tools/mint_artefact.mjs <instance> <run>`), which writes a κ-labelled artefact whose address re-derives from its bytes, and carry the artefact, the run directory and the chronicle to the City's Exchange or to a pull request against the origin. A derived edge proposes; only a signature mints. Nothing here publishes on your behalf.

## Naming it in public

If a result of yours goes onto a public board or into a paper, the method is what makes the claim a measurement, so name it:

```
Harness: <your runtime> with the agentprivacy dual-agent harness (<your instance> instance)
```

and say in a sentence what the seated loop did: a proposing seat planned one lever; a hold-apart seat drew the witnesses by hashing the proposal; a proving seat measured and returned a verdict; a critic classified what closed. Killed levers were filed, not retried.
